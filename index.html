How it works

  1. Sign in with Discord or your email.
  2. Join: accept the code of conduct, tell us your average, and send a photo of your board.
  3. Placement: play 3 matches so we can put you in the right division.
  4. League: one match a week for 10 weeks. Your opponent is revealed every Monday.
  5. Challenges: play anyone for fun. Tick Coach me to learn from a stronger player.

All you need is a board, darts and a camera pointed at the board. It's free. Always.

Blocks and reports
